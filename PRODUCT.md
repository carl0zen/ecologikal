# Ecologikal — Product Context

register: brand + product (dual)
Last updated: 2026-07-30

## Product purpose

Ecologikal is a regenerative eco-social network: an operating system for ecological participation. People earn reputation through a peer-attested **skill flower**, navigate six **intent pillars**, contribute to **eco-centers** and places, and settle value with **KINS** — not vanity metrics.

Spanish-first (Monterrey origins), GEN-adjacent petal taxonomy. Revival lives under `apps/web` + `packages/domain`; vintage PHP is archive.

## Users

| Persona | Need |
|---------|------|
| Econauta | Show skills, find peers, earn KINS, travel with purpose |
| Eco-center host | Run venue culture, workshops, vacancies, bookings |
| Volunteer / cooperante | Match petal skills to real work |
| Learner | Useful knowledge filtered by petal, not noise |
| Steward | Protect ecozonas; surface and fund local needs |

## Brand voice (summary)

**Thesis:** La flor es tu reputación. / Your flower is your reputation.

Voice is **field-useful, peer-attested, compost-warm** — never startup hype, never new-age fluff, never Certexi industrial coldness. Full system: `/brand-system` and `docs/BRAND_SYSTEM.md`.

## Strategic principles

1. Petals are the category OS — never invent a parallel taxonomy.
2. Flower > résumé. Peer references grade competence.
3. Intent modes (Juega · Viaja · Descubre · Aprende · Conoce · Coopera) beat generic Home/Feed shells.
4. Amplificate ≠ Broadcast ≠ Like.
5. Eco-center is a first-class venue, not a Facebook Page.
6. Separate brand from Certexi (platform OS). Same proof plane, different identity.

## Anti-references

- Generic green SaaS / "save the planet" startup kitsch
- Facebook/LinkedIn chrome with eco stickers
- Purple-indigo AI gradients, cream+terracotta editorial cliché
- Certexi graphite+signal lime (sibling OS — not our look)
- Hippie rainbow chaos or cyberpunk neon nature
- Exclamation-mark marketing and empty mission adjectives

## Differentiation

| Pattern elsewhere | Ecologikal |
|-------------------|------------|
| Likes / followers | Amplificate · Broadcast · flower grade |
| Résumé skills | 7-petal flower + peer references |
| Infinite scroll | Six intent feeds |
| Points vanity | KINS as spendable ecosocial currency |
| Meetup listing | Eco-center ops + skill-matched cooperate |

## Flagship & registers (blend addendum)

- **Agroabundanza Institute** is the flagship eco-center: a 330-hectare family
  institute (Regenerar · Educar · Elevar) whose retreats put people to work on
  real social/ecological problems. The landing features it; the demo seed
  provisions it.
- **Registers:** home and login are brand surfaces (cinematic, Netflix-like
  storytelling in service of impact); the six pillar screens are product
  surfaces. Scene sentence: *a guest under a palapa at golden hour (light); an
  econauta in a candle-lit common room at night (dark).* Both themes are
  first-class; the stored choice wins.
